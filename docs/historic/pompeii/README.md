# Pompeii: design language

Pompeii is read on **a summer morning of 79 CE**, before the eruption. The scene covers the western half of the
town: the forum and the old town round it, the Triangular Forum and the theatres on the south bluff, the
Stabian Baths, Via dell'Abbondanza east to Via Stabiana, and the Porta Marina climb. It should read as Roman
at a glance:
- a podium temple at the end of a forum, with Vesuvius behind it;
- low red tile roofs over painted stucco;
- dark lava streets between raised pavements, with stepping stones at the crossings;
- shopfronts open to the main streets and blank house walls on the lanes.

The town is also mid-repair. Seventeen years after the earthquake of 62/63 CE, the Capitolium and the Temple of
Venus are being rebuilt and the Central Baths are unfinished.

The numbers below are the kit's design language. The machine copy, checked once per principle, is the style card
[`control/lib/graph/historic/style/pompeii.js`](../../../control/lib/graph/historic/style/pompeii.js). When the
two disagree, the card wins. Facts belong in the record (`record/pompeii.js`), which carries their citations.

## Kit constants

What the record settles and what is a design number. The record entry is named in brackets.

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Podium temple | Podium 2.5–3.5 m; the Capitolium's is about 3 m on a 37 × 17 m plan (`capitolium`). One stair, at the front only; a pediment |
| Orders | Shaft height in lower diameters: Tuscan and Doric 7, Ionic 9, Corinthian 10 (Vitruvius). The upper order of a two-storey portico is 0.75 of the lower: the forum's is Doric under Ionic (`forum-portico-tuff`) |
| Roof | Terracotta tegulae and imbrices at about 22°, eave 0.4–0.8 m. **A design number**: no Pompeian pitch was found (`tile-roof`, `roof-tile` unverified) |
| Street | Lava paving between kerbed pavements 0.3–0.5 m high; stepping stones 0.3 m high, 0.9 × 0.5 m, with gaps for a 1.4 m cart gauge (design numbers, `kerbed-street` unverified). Via dell'Abbondanza is 6.7 m wide east of Via Stabiana and 14.6 m wide at its western end, narrowing to 7.4 m; it stands 0.4 m above Via Stabiana, so carts cannot turn west (`via-abbondanza`) |
| Walls | A dark socle 0.9–1.2 m at the foot of a painted wall, a lighter field above (the kit's reading of the Third Style's plain fields; no source states it as a convention). Storeys 3.5–4.5 m |
| Palette | Stucco, Sarno limestone, Nocera tufa, lava, tile, brick, cocciopesto, red, yellow, black. **Estimated** from the record's materials until the swatch sheet exists |

## The town at 79 CE

Each building in the record carries a `state` at the read year, and the scene draws it that way:

- **Damaged, awaiting work:** the Capitolium; the Macellum, with its portico unbuilt.
- **Under repair:** the Temple of Apollo; the Eumachia building; the forum square, whose repaving is unfinished.
- **Unfinished new work:** the Temple of Venus; the travertine forum colonnade; the Central Baths (outside the frame).
- **Repaired:** the Temple of Isis, the only temple wholly rebuilt; the Large Theatre's stage; the Stabian Baths.
- **Relic:** the Doric Temple in the Triangular Forum; the walls, more monument than defence by now.

The gaps a later pass should close:
- the Large Theatre's diameter and the Quadriporticus's size;
- the forum portico heights, and the widths of Via Stabiana and Via Marina;
- the bluff's height and the insula count;
- any statement of Pompeian roof pitch or tile size.

## Built

The town is laid out before its pieces are designed, so the drawing brief can be checked against what it needs
(`control/lib/graph/historic/layouts/pompeii.js`, culture `pompeii`, layout `lava-spur`). Every piece is a
placeholder in the kit (`assets/pompeii.js`, all `designed: false`):
- **Massing blocks** in the card's numbers: podium temple, public hall, two-storey forum portico, colonnaded
  court, arch, gate, fountain.
- **Borrowed as they stand from Lindos:** the courtyard house (less its Greek porch) as the atrium house's
  stand-in, the wall, towers, the theatre, statues.

A building's state at 79 shows on its block. Damaged and relic stand roofless; unfinished stands part-built with
scaffolding; under repair keeps its roof and has scaffolding.

The asset call (`assetCall`) says what the town asks for: one Capitolium, three other podium temples, three
forum porticoes, about a dozen halls, three courts, about 700 houses with shops on the main streets, two gates,
and the pieces borrowed from Lindos. The sheets above cover them all. The tombs and the Villa of the Mysteries are
outside this frame, so `pompeii-tombs.webp` can wait.

The positions follow the town plan's order, not a survey. The streets are drawn orthogonal, and the bluff's
height (18 m) is drawn, since the record has none. The World page is about 15 MB.

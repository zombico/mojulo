## The ground

Its share of a level: averaged over the cube presets' stage land, each with its own base, grit and formation; dry
as the presets lay it, by the water a word lays (lake, coast, river: era/out-water.js, averaged), and a level's,
50% of the levels by the water and the rest dry.

| substrate | what it is | dry | by the water | share of a level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| soil | earth deep enough to root in: the turf and the woods | 73.8% | 61.4% | 67.6% |
| sand | loose grains, dry and shifting: a beach, a dune, a sandstone flat | 0.0% | 6.7% | 3.4% |
| wet | ground the water keeps: a stream bank, a marsh, a seep | 0.0% | 6.9% | 3.4% |
| path | ground walked bare: the trail and its edge | 10.0% | 9.9% | 10.0% |

A cube's rock formation turns its soil: limestone (thin soil over limestone gives way to bare pavement); sandstone (sandstone weathers to sand on its flats); flagstone (slabs of the bed break through the turf).

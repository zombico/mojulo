build 37eefe7b

Open a page in the Quest browser and press vr (top left, beside walk) straight away: VR starts you where the page's view stands, on the course's lane facing the blue goal. Left stick walks and strafes where your body points. Seated, the right stick turns your body; standing, you turn it yourself. Your head just looks. Holding the Meta button recenters.

Check: a long push forward stays on the lane while you look around; after a right-stick turn, forward is the new way; turning happens where you stand; the turn speed feels right.

course

→ `sketch_what_possible` knob-resolution loop → `create_sketch({recipe:{kind,...}})`. Single-shot natural language → `mint_solid` kind `manji-tree` `via:'packet'` (key-free); a landscape, a nature scene, a place → card `scene` (the geocube's land, walkable); cardinal-grammar structural illustration → `create_manji_tree` (2D or 3D). (Contrast: a diagram/chart → `create_sketch` directly; a posed human body → `create_figure`.) Full family → `get_creative_toolset({ form: 'illustration' })`.

# Gentle Pulse

Soft rolling terrain typical of meadows, fairways, and open pasture. Two
low-frequency sine components produce broad swells with subtle long-wave
modulation. Best paired with the `meadow-trio`, `verdure-trio`, or
`dusk-trio` splatches.

The amplitudes (1.0–1.6, 0.5–0.8) sit in the low band — peaks rise gently
above the ground plane without dramatic crests. The cycles ranges keep
the wave count low across the quad, so the eye reads broad hills rather
than rapid ripples.

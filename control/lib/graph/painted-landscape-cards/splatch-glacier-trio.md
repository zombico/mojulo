# Glacier Trio

Cold austerity palette: deep slate-blue shadow → cool grey-blue mid →
near-white highlight. The narrow hue spread (all cool) and high contrast
give scenes a glacial / arctic / overcast feel. Pairs naturally with
`chop` for cold water and `ridge-step` for snowy ridges.

# Firelight Trio

Warm nocturnal palette: deep umber shadow → fired-clay base → amber
highlight. Reads as scene lit by fire or torchlight against a dark
ground. Pairs naturally with `breathing` for night dunes, `ridge-step`
for warm sunset terraces, and `chop` for water under firelight.

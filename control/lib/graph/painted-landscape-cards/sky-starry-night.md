# Starry Night

A clear, cloudless night strewn with faint stars across the upper sky,
deterministically scattered and denser toward the zenith. Stars are
**night-gated**: they only appear once the sun is down, fading in as the
day→night arc crosses into darkness. Set a NEGATIVE `light.z` (the sun below
the horizon) so the scene reads as night — under a daytime light this card
renders a plain clear sky. Combine with `harvest-moon` knobs for a moonlit
night.

# Harvest Moon

A moonlit night: a pale moon disc with a soft halo, riding high opposite the
sun's azimuth, over a thinner scatter of stars. Both are **night-gated** —
they appear only once the sun has set, so set a NEGATIVE `light.z`. The
`phase` knob (1 full → 0 new) carves the moon toward a crescent; this card
ships a gibbous `0.82`. The ground already takes a cool moonlit tint from the
sky's night arc, so the moon completes the read rather than lighting the
scene.

# Broken Overcast

Heavy cover with a deliberate clearing — a sun-gap where the sky opens and
the light pours through. The `break` is the dual of a terrain basin: it
carves a hole in the cloud field. Place the break near the light's azimuth
(`x` toward the sun) for a god-rays read.

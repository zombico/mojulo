# Ridge Step

High-amplitude waves with relatively high v-cycles — produces sharp
ridges receding in depth, the terraced-field read. Good for stepped
agricultural landscapes (rice terraces, vineyards) or sharp geological
features. The light direction tilts toward camera so the front face of
each ridge catches highlight. Best paired with `verdure-trio` for rice
terraces, `firelight-trio` for warm sunset on the fields, or `bone-trio`
for ancient stone terraces.

# Chart Primary

Deep textbook blue → vermilion red → bone white. The classic mid-century
textbook / scholastic chart aesthetic — high-contrast primaries on a
near-white field. Good for "diagram" or "math cover" feel. Pairs with
`topographic` for chart-like contour borders; pairs with `painterly`
for a softer textbook-illustration read.

# Velvet Cinema

Inky midnight blue → muted velvet violet → antique gold highlight.
Cinematic / noir aesthetic — feels lit by a single warm source against
a deep field. Pairs naturally with `painterly` renderStyle and elevated
`gamma` (≥ 1.3) in `paletteOverrides` to deepen the midtones into
dramatic atmosphere.

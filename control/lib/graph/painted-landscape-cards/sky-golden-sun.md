# Golden Sun

A cloudless sky carrying the sun itself — a warm luminary disc with a soft
radial halo at the light's azimuth. The disc rides the diurnal arc: white-hot
under a high sun, sunset-orange as `light.z` drops toward the horizon, and it
disappears entirely once the sun sets (a negative `light.z` is night — reach
for `starry-night` or `harvest-moon` there). Pair with a low, warm `light`
(small positive `z`) for the classic golden-hour glow.

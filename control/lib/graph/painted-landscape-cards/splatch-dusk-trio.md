# Dusk Trio

Sunset drama palette: deep navy shadow → violet midtone → warm coral
highlight. The cool-to-warm sweep gives scenes a dramatic sky-lit feel
without explicit sky geometry — the ground reads as lit from a warm
low sun. Pairs naturally with `chop` for sunset water or `gentle-pulse`
for evening fields.

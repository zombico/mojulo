# Breathing

Medium-amplitude waves with a slow envelope — the dune-like read.
Lower-frequency components than `gentle-pulse` with higher amplitude
ranges, plus a side-rake default light to make the lee slopes shadow
naturally. Best paired with `bone-trio` or `firelight-trio` splatches
for desert / arid scenes.

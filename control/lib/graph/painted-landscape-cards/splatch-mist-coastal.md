# Mist Coastal

Slate shadow → muted teal mid → pale fog highlight. Low-chroma palette
for misty coastal, overcast cinematic, or any scene where atmosphere
should dominate over local color. Pairs with `chop` for foggy water,
`glacial-smooth` for windswept tundra, `gentle-roughness` for moorland.

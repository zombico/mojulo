### A platformer's first level, made from its hero's tuned motion

- **Planned.** A platformer world's first level is five trail segments joined at seams: the basic movement, the first
  encounter, the advanced movement, a gauntlet of foes on platforming ground, and a boss on a story-driven game. Each is
  sized by what its feel's card can cross and played with the card's own takes, fitted to its frames.

### A platformer's first level, made from its hero's tuned motion

- **Planned.** A platformer world's first level is trail segments joined at seams: the basic movement, the first
  encounter, the advanced movement, and a gauntlet of foes on platforming ground. Each is sized by what its feel's card
  can cross and played with the card's own takes, fitted to its frames. It plays from the side (the camera and the
  movement locked to the trail's line) or in full 3D, on the same level lit and dressed as every game's.
- **A boss when the creator asks.** The level's requisition adds the boss and its fight, and the win is written as one
  of three kinds: a jobber (thrown away), a rival or a boss (each a story hook). The kind changes nothing in the fight; the
  next level's build reads it, and the log keeps how the story accrues.

### The World page's light: one record, the sky in every reflection, a finished frame

- **Under way, nothing shipped yet.** The World page keeps its baked light and gains, opt-in and adding no byte to a
  page that does not ask: one light record (sun, sky, ambient) every lit channel reads; one capture of the page's own
  sky as what metal, specular faces and water reflect, and rooms that reflect the room; and a frame drawn in linear
  light and finished in the frame pass (bloom, exposure that adapts, a tone curve whose highlights roll off to white,
  dither), with the grade laid over it as today.

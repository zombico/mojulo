### Skandart: debris that has a cause, laid where it would come to rest

A room gathers what has happened to it: flakes and spall at a wall's foot under its failing coat, wax under the candles,
webs in the angles above reach. Skandart asks a room what falls, wears off, is dropped or grows there and where it comes
to rest, and lays the answer as clumps: built once, stamped where the room's structure says they lie, each taking the
light of its own spot. Smaller than a hand, debris is relief in the floor's tile; bigger, it is built at the size it
would be.

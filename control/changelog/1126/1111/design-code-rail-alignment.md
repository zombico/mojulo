### Design and code rails aligned: a game made from a sentence is one a player can win

Two action games were made end to end from a sentence (a stylish action game and an anime action RPG), exported and
played by a scripted player on the shipped pages. Every level built and loaded and every action piece ran, and no level
could be won: the rails wrote what the runtime does not play, and the hero, its blows and its foes were numbered in three
scales. Designed, not built: nothing below is in the code yet.

- **A player gate.** A made level is played headless by a scripted player on the runtime it ships (lock on, close, the
  kit's first string, guard, the sidearm on a foe out of reach); design_game's readout carries whether it was won, in
  how long and by whom the hero was hit, and a level lost is advised, never refused. Every level page of a made game is
  loaded in headless Chrome and must boot.
- **The page binds what its legend says.** A moveset's mouse buttons strike on every camera, not only a mouse-look one;
  two channels no longer declare the same page-level name (a hero with a power's look on a level with lock-on threw on
  load).
- **The sentence door reads the genre.** "Action rpg" and "stylish action" name their genres; a longer name beats a
  shorter one inside it ("rpg"). The composer lays the orbit camera a genre's cube asks for.
- **One scale.** In a world that writes an arsenal, the kit's blows, the hero's health and each foe's hit are numbered in
  the foe shelf's frame, so a foe falls in the strings its rank means and a hit costs what its class deals. A world with
  no arsenal keeps every byte.
- **The casting call reads the kit and the place.** A ranged foe is cast only where the hero carries a sidearm that
  reaches it; a melee game's cast fights in melee; a town stands no foes unless the world says so.
- **The rails say what they wrote.** A changed genre is answered with what the composer laid for the old one (a party,
  its battles) and the patch that removes it; the readout's loop names how each level is won.

### The geocube card: its presets in groups, accurate to what a cube takes

- The geocube card names its presets a group a line (a level's look, a frame's open terrain, a frame's industrial
  interior, the children of Sol), each group described on a card of its own (`geocube/<group>`), so a new preset grows
  its group's card and never the geocube's: the card is back under its ceiling with room to spare.
- It no longer lists each preset's ingredients (its light, bones and land), which read as what a preset is for and
  misled (a smelter hall's "research-lab bones").
- It says what it was missing: every specimen `show` takes (not only room and land), a cube's `light.heavens` and its
  interiors, a field's standing rock (`massif`) and a works' hall over its land (`works`), and the recipe's `sea`.

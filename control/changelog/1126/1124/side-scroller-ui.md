### Side-scrollers' UI: the castle's HUD on mana-ui, looks for each place, and the HUD heard

- Vampire Sonata's HUD is drawn on mana-ui in a castle look of its own, the `nocturne` card: aged gold on black, a
  serif, a Victorian cresting, health in crimson and magic in blue. It shows her vitals and the place she stands in;
  a banner across the top for each thing found (a power held longer, with its own bell), "Saved", a waking, a new
  region, a boss's name and its fall; the boss's bar while it stands; and what she holds. A pause (Enter) has the map
  of the rooms walked, her vitals and the castle's relics, found or locked.
- Re-keyed looks: `nocturne-frost` and `nocturne-blood` for the castle under frost and a blood moon, and `galaxy-neon`
  and `galaxy-orbit` for the side map's neon city and orbital colony. A level wears the one for its box's theme or its
  setting.
- A level's HUD on mana-ui is heard in its look's UI sounds: banners chime, a rare one rings, menus open and close and
  the cursor clicks. The castle in the crypt set, the side map in chip. Muted with the game's sound; a silent look adds
  nothing.

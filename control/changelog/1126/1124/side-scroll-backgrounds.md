### Side-scrollers' backgrounds: contrast, composition, light and motion

- What makes a side-scroller's background nice to look at, written down as principles (`docs/side-scroll-backgrounds.md`):
  the play owns the picture's extremes; distance is told by hue; the rows stand in a stair of silhouettes and leave the
  sky its share; the light's source is in the picture; and something moves on most screens, slowly, a little and
  never in step.
- A new page channel moves a backdrop: `ambient` names render groups that sway, twinkle or blink on the page's clock,
  each phased by where it stands. A page without it carries no bytes of it.
- Jumping Giuseppe's strip: the hill rows lowered into a stair, so the sky takes a quarter to a third of the frame; the
  far rows' lit caps quieter than the near ones'; the big domes cut finer; the terrace a step into the air, so the lane
  reads against it; clouds on a shaded underside, drifting; the blossom stirring.
- Vampire Sonata's castle: the night over the ramparts has stars that twinkle, cloud wisps that drift, a haloed moon,
  far mountains and a forest ridge before the towers' feet. The night's bands no longer lap: lapped bands were lifted
  a step each by the page and stood in front of the towers high up. Every window shows the night, pale at its foot,
  with a moonbeam falling from it to the floor that breathes as cloud passes. Torches, candles and braziers burn as live
  flames, and the cold lamps pulse.
- Galaxy Boy's and Astro Girl's plant: a vista under the sky stands against a dusk glow drawn in bands, so its towers
  and stacks read as silhouettes. Towers carry blinking beacons and masts, and the middle skyline has lit windows.
  Stacks steam and gantries carry amber lamps. A hall's far wall has a row of lit clerestory windows.
- Galaxy Boy's level is drawn in a city by night of its own instead of the mascot platformer's orchard, and each of the
  plant's places has its own colour (teal, azure, indigo, violet), so the stage is no longer one brown all through.
  Two new places, the reactor gallery and the sky bridge, keep the gauntlet from repeating the gate tunnel.
- A future city in the plant's vistas: domed arcologies with lit window bands, glass domes lit from inside, needle
  towers under saucer decks, neon down the near buildings, a skyway whose train runs and craft drifting between the
  layers.
- A giant set piece: the reactor, the machine the plant makes, a wheel larger than the window turning slowly about a
  pulsing core, an inner ring turning the other way, pistons pumping before it. It is seen in the reactor gallery and
  again in the core. The page's ambient channel learned to spin a group about its hub.
- The castle's rooms are dressed as locales by what they are: every save room a chapel of stained glass with a rose
  window over its altar; the great halls' shafts the library's stacks under an orrery whose worlds turn; the inner
  ward's shafts and the Bellkeeper's room a brick clock tower, its gears meshing and the great clock's hands going round;
  every other corridor of the ward the catacombs, rough rock, skulls in their niches and a sarcophagus; the Lord's room
  a throne room. The backs are laid in their own walls (ashlar, brick, rock, plaster), and every backdrop law still
  holds.
- The night's clouds are banks you can see, puffed and lit on the moon's side, and one crosses the moon.
- Jumping Giuseppe's strip runs through sceneries, a trail each: the orchard it starts in, a lake (water in bands,
  the far hills reflected, light glinting, a sailing boat drifting), a forest's edge (a mint canopy behind a blossom
  one, swaying) and the coast (the sea to the horizon, islands, a sand beach). A headland stands where the scenery
  changes. A level names them as its `style.scenery`.
- What made the three backdrops starters, written down (`docs/backdrop-variations.md`): a place is a row, a piece a
  word, and the deal matches them by role; the slots every backdrop fills; the principles of composition, colour
  keying (the cube keys the light, the place its hue within a band set apart from play, the lit things the accent),
  light and motion that let a variation be new words; the pitfalls the page taught; and how to add a scenery, a place,
  a vista or a locale.
- Seeded variations, Giuseppe's: dunes (warm sand hills, a beach, teal cacti in the groves) and a snowfield (the hills
  under snow, a snow terrace, frosted crowns), each a re-key of the orchard's rows with no drawing of its own.
- Seeded variations, Galaxy Boy's: every side map setting's places have their own tones; three vistas built from the
  existing pieces (a neon market, an orbit with stars and a planet rising under the window, a greenhouse of domed
  gardens); two settings dealt from them, a neon city and an orbital colony. Every side map law holds in every setting.
- Seeded variations, the castle's: two re-keys, castle-frost (an ice-blue night, a pale moon, cold lamps) and
  castle-blood (a blood moon, crimson halls), each the same rings and locales in other colours with no drawing of its
  own; two locales from new pieces, the alchemist's laboratory (potions glowing on their shelves, a cauldron bubbling
  under its hood) in every other corridor of the halls, and the armoury (weapon racks, a suit of armour) in the keep's
  other hall. Every law holds in every theme.
- The castle's fabric (`docs/metroidvania-map.md` "The castle's fabric"): the parts Vampire Sonata's hero stands on, runs
  into and passes through are built as what they are, where they were grey blocks. A floor is a path in its room's
  material (flagstones, boards, a checker of tiles, the rock's own beds) over a string course and coursed stone; a
  ceiling a cornice on a corbel table, a timber hoarding over the walks outside, machicolations at the top; a wall
  quoined, with a plinth, an impost and a string course at each storey. Every doorway has an arched head, a threshold
  and the passage's far reveal seen through it; every hole through a floor is framed. A stair's ledges are stone slabs,
  timber platforms hung on rods from what is over them, or iron catwalks on a truss, by the room. The towers on the
  box's edge have an arrow slit each storey; moss and damp weather the stone outside. The box has a crown (a parapet,
  bartizans, corner towers under conical roofs) and stands on a crag. Each is a law (`fabric:*`), and every law holds
  in every theme.
- The backdrop shots frame a castle room as the play does (a cell, the zone holding the frame), and `--whole` shows the
  castle entire.

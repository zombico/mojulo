### More to do in the city

- Four new driving jobs on Jobsified. **Ambulance calls**: take one of the emergency station's ambulances, pick up the
  patient waiting by a red cross sign and drop them off. **Fire calls**: take a fire engine to fires burning at the kerb
  and hold to hose each one down. **Courier run**: a courier van from the dock, a parcel handed over at each of four
  doors. **Mail round**: a mail truck emptying four post boxes, the mail taken back to the yard. The stations' jobs show
  up where a district has an emergency station.
- Each job's screen line speaks its own words (a patient aboard, "hold F to hose it down") instead of a bus's.
- Fixed: the heavy vehicle call, street sweeping and the new mail round could be silently missing when their yard had
  no kerb nearby; their vehicle now waits at a kerb near the start instead. A street with a streetcar line no longer
  loses all its kerb parking. The courier's van never parks in a bay another truck already stands in.
- Two more buildings to walk into near the start: an **arcade** behind a lit pink-and-cyan sign, its games floor with a
  blocks cabinet, and a **public library** with a small portico, a reading room with a match table, the stacks and the
  archive in plaster and oak. Each opens on a page of its own, like the museum, and you walk back out onto its steps.
- An **aquarium** behind a deep blue sign: glass tanks of water down its three wings, The Reef, Life on the Bed and Open
  Water, with clownfish, angelfish, a pufferfish and a cuttlefish, an octopus and a moray eel on the sand, and a sea
  turtle, a manta ray and squid in the big tank at the end.
- A **zoo** behind a gate with a lit sign and a ticket booth: open to the sky, three wings of pens fenced in mesh,
  The Savanna (zebras, ostriches, a giraffe and an elephant in the big enclosure), Big Cats and Bears (lions, tigers, a
  brown bear and a polar bear) and Around the World (kangaroos hopping, flamingos, penguins, meerkats and camels).
- **Hidden stars**: ten stars tucked round Hijack City where nobody walks on the way anywhere (a park's far corner,
  the back of an empty lot or a building site, beside a highway ramp's foot, the corners of the dock and the yards),
  spread far apart and picked up into the bag. Any world can hide some (`hidden: true | { stars, item }`).

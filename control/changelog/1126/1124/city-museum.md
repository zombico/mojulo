### A museum in the city

- Hijack City has a museum: a stone building with columns and banners on a block near where you start. Walk up its
  steps and through its door and you are inside, on its own page, without the city loaded behind it; walk back out and
  you are on its steps again, the city as you left it.
- Inside: a lobby and three gallery wings, each with its own name: plaster walls (mostly white, an oxblood or a
  grey-blue room here and there), oak and pale stone floors, daylight from the ceiling and warm lights. Each piece stands
  on a plinth, and one piece per wing stands full size in the hall at the wing's end. Nothing to win, just a place to
  look around.
- The museum is filled with things made in mojulo: cars and a tram, a digger, swords and a shield, animals from a
  wolf to an elephant, and furniture.

### Warlords of Mars heard: the recorded library first

- Every operation now has sound. A frame is heard by its parts: its footfalls and landings by its legs, its thrust,
  ignition and quick boost by its booster, each gun by what it is (the carbine's heavy rounds, the gatling's bursts,
  the lance's charge and beam, the bazooka, the Hornet's salvo, the Arc's discharge, the Shear's blade), its cockpit's
  lock-on, EN alarm and vent, and the power-up as it drops. A part fitted in the hangar changes how it sounds.
- Its foes too: turrets, carriers, Bastions behind their shields, drones and gunships humming overhead, each struck,
  each going up; bursts where rounds land; the objectives' stingers; Mars's wind outside and the works' furnaces in.
- Every word is the recorded library's first (Mech Battle Kai's suit kit, the registry's machines, the explosions),
  its synthesized voice where the library is not installed. The sounds the library lacks are new rows heard as their
  stand-ins until they are recorded, and their cards are written (`cards/warlords-of-mars.js`).
- The hangar's and the briefing's menus play the holo table's sound set.
- The recorded `shield-hit` card (a sword on a wooden shield) voices a parry's block now, not an energy shield.

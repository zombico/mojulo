### Blaze Gunner: a scrolling shooter in space, left to right

The start of Blaze Gunner, an original horizontal scrolling shooter: one small, agile ship, a screen that scrolls on its
own, waves, a hull flown along and a capital-ship boss. Its game is its weapons:
- pods taken from the foes that carry them;
- each pod gives the ship a different hurtbox, a shape in time;
- attacking and dodging over shielding;
- eight-way movement on the d-pad;
- damage taken from HP.

It is laid in the side map's 2.5D. Play happens on one plane, and space stands behind it in layers that parallax under
a perspective camera. Shots are drawn as light, and the light they spill on the hulls is laid over a baked key. Every
piece is written in screen axes, so a vertical stage seen from above is a change of basis.

Nothing is built yet.

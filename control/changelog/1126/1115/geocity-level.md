### A city district as a game level

- **A game's level can be a district of a city.** A level can now be one neighbourhood of a city, drawn in detail
  with the rest of the city as its skyline, and walked by the game's hero from its streets. Where the city's shops
  are open the hero walks into them, and an errand can be traded at a shop's counter there.
- **A city page is lighter.** A city's cars, cranes and site machines are now stored in the same compact form as the
  shop interiors. A test district's page went from about 72 MB to about 43 MB in the painted look (from 149 MB to 120 MB
  in the default look, where the land and plants are most of the weight).

### City stalker on the geocity: a superhero's city, swung through in metres

- **A game level can be a whole city neighbourhood.** A game's level may now be a piece of a metro (its towers,
  streets, parks and waterfront at true size), the same city the studio already draws as a skyline. Until now only a
  town could be a level; a city could be looked at and walked, not played as a game's level.
- **The hero swings and climbs there.** A hero who starts with the wall climb and the swing line uses them on that
  city's buildings: hold the swing key in the air to cast a line to a tower's face and swing down the street, press it
  on the ground to zip up to a wall, climb any face to its roof.

### Hijack City movers: a house emptied into a moving truck

- **Movers**: a removals job on Jobsified in Hijack City. Take the moving truck from the dock to the Hendersons' house on
  the block, go in, and empty it: any piece (a couch, a fridge, a box) is lifted whole with E and carried in your hands,
  through any door, up and down the stairs, out of the basement, nothing it would hit stopping it. Carried to the front
  door it goes on the truck; twelve fill it, and it pays. (docs/movers.md)
- A family's house to walk into (`house`): two storeys over a basement, furnished as a home is (a sofa and armchairs in
  the living rooms, a fridge and a dining table in the kitchen-diners, beds and wardrobes upstairs, boxes in the
  basement), and new household pieces on the furniture shelf: sofa, armchair, coffee and dining tables, bookcase, media
  console, chest of drawers, nightstand, fridge, wardrobe, moving box.
- Lifting is a runtime piece any stage can ask for (`heave`): a held piece's box leaves the world while it is carried,
  and its drawing rides in the hands.
- A stage over a basement now puts its front door, the arriving walker and its drop zone on the floor its way in is on.

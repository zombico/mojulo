### City sound: the open city heard

- **The city has a sound of its own.** A game on the sound sheet (`sound: {}`) hears a played town or district as a
  street: sparrows and birds on the ledges (a new `city` bed, also asked for as street, town or downtown). It has no
  cars in it: car sounds come only from the car you drive. Footsteps, the punches, a crew's shouts and grunts, and the score (`music: {}`) with the fight cue coming
  in as a fight starts all come from what the game already is.
- **The cars are heard, but only at the wheel.** A car's engine rises and falls with the speed of the car you are
  driving, and is silent on foot. A car door sounds as you get in or out, H sounds the horn, and hitting something at
  speed sounds a crash.
- **The jobs are heard.** Taking a job from someone, a call with the next one (two pips, until the shelf has a phone
  ring), a TaskFu task, subscribing, joining a faction, taking a posting, finishing it, a fight starting and going
  down each have a sound.
- **Lighter street people.** Each passer-by is now packed (each corner once, weights a byte each): a person is about
  a quarter of the size, so a street of them costs less than it did before they were cleaned up.
- **Footsteps on a street are a heel's click.** A played town or district is walked on its pavement whatever the rest
  of the world grows, so its steps are a new pavement footstep (a clean click and a knock, barely a scuff) in place of
  the grass rustle it was getting. No footsteps sound while you are in a car (or riding anything); the car's engine
  is what you hear, and it is louder than before. The engine runs only while you are in the car: it starts as you get
  in and stops as you get out, with no idle hum while you walk.
- **You can hear the car.** The car's engine was pitched like a real car's, too low for a laptop's or a phone's
  speaker to play. Cars in the city now have an engine voiced for small speakers (a new road-car engine: its note
  higher, a burble, the tyres' hiss growing with speed), and it changes up through its gears as you pull away instead
  of one long rising whine.

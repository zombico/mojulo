### The open city's UI, drawn as an open-world game's

The open city's screens worked, but they read as an app. The radar was a square picture in a yellow frame, money was a
boxed "coin 0", and a strip of every key sat along the bottom of the screen at all times. The map, phone, bag and
journal were huge solid-yellow panels over a striped backdrop. They now read as a city game at night. This work is UI
only: nothing about how the city plays has changed.

- **A city look.** A new look, Downtown: near-black glass, white words, a taxi's yellow for what matters and a
  banknote's green for money. Nothing is drawn across the screen, so the city shows through the map, the phone, the bag
  and the journal. A game made as a city game starts from it.
- **The radar** is round, with a rim and a shadow, and fades toward its edge. The opened map shows the city at night
  with water in blue, and names only where to go and where you are (every stop's long label at once was unreadable).
- **Money** is a coin and a large figure, with no box and no word. The bag shows it the same way.
- **Keys appear when they matter.** The strip of keys speaks when what you can do changes (a car in reach, the wheel
  taken, a call on the phone) and steps away after a few seconds.
- **The car's name.** Getting into a car says its name large at the bottom right ("MID SUV"), then it is gone.
- **The objective** is one line with a mark and the distance.
- **The phone is a phone.** It is a handset held at the bottom right, with the city still visible around it. It has the
  hour and the signal along its top, its apps as tabs, and the contacts above the open conversation.

### The open city's guns: drawn or put away, and the police armed from the third star

- **A holster** (`rule.holster`, laid on the bare hands' pistol where a city sells rounds: `arsenal.js`): the pistol is
  drawn or put away on R (the weapon cycle) or by number, 1 the hands and 2 the gun. Put away, the click punches and
  the gun fires not at all; drawn, the click fires it at the foe before the hero and swings nothing (Q held too). The
  page's reticle and ammo panel show its rounds. A sidearm with no holster fires on Q as it always did.
- **Armed police** (`law.armed`, the third star unless said, or false): from that star the police come as the tactical
  unit in black, a pistol each (`LAW.gun`, a loadout slot before their blow), firing at the player in sight; their
  rounds are blows on the street's health.

### The open city's sound: ambient beats, the street, and a car you hear

- **Ambient beats under the streets** (`cue-sheet.js` `cityScore`, a played city in a game with `music`): the club's
  `chill` (boom-bap with a swing, tape and dust, keys in sevenths and ninths) on the game's one identity, in place of the
  land's orchestral cue; where the law or a faction can come for the player, a chase (`drive`) the level changes to as
  the tension rises (a star, a fight, blows), held while any star is on (the wanted level's calm raises it each second).
- **The street** (`sfx-shelf/beds.js` `city`): horns far off down the blocks, a truck, a dog, a siren somewhere, the wind
  between the towers, sparrows; and under it the city's **traffic far off** (`traffic-hum`, the new `ambience` held family,
  the audio channel's `ambience`), quieter from inside a car.
- **The car's sound design** (`sound-sheet.js` `citySounds`, the audio channel's cab): the door opened as the driver
  reaches it and shut as they sit, the starter cranking and the engine catching a moment later (`car-ignition`); the
  engine through its gears, its **growl under the pedal** (`road-car-load`, silent coasting), its **tyres' roar and the
  wind** by the speed (`road-roar`), their **screech** past their grip in a turn or under hard braking, the **brakes**
  squealing as it stops hard (`brake-squeal`), a **gear** changed up under the pedal; getting out, the engine stopped
  (`car-engine-off`), the door opened and shut. The audio read takes an engine's `drive` (held voices beside it) and a
  level's `ambience`.
- **Fixed**: the city's engine never started. While a car is driven the pilot is the car, which rides nothing: the sound
  now finds the driver as the car's rider.

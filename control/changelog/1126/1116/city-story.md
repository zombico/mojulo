### The open city's story: missions with ways to fail

- **A job told as a story mission** (an errand's `mission`: `{ heat?, clean?, time?, final? }`, and its `name`;
  `game/mission-mechanics.js`): it starts as the job is taken (its name as a banner, a clock when it has time), its goods
  can bring the law's stars (`heat`), its hand-in can ask for none (`clean`: lose the police first), and it fails when
  the player is wasted, busted or out of time: MISSION FAILED, then it starts again a few seconds on. Handed in, MISSION
  PASSED; the story's last (`final`) ends the level, the game won. A contact's missions come one after another by phone.
- **A one-off's hand-in is quiet once done**: coming back to a giver who has had it asks nothing more of it.

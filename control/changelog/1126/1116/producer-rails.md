### The producer's rails: the size and the template picked before anything is built

Planned; nothing here has shipped yet. Each line is added as its phase lands.

- **The producer faces the person and the director builds**, one agent in both seats. The producer knows the shape of
  what mojulo makes (a line a rail), decides how much of it a game needs (a pixelizer game, a quick game or the full
  design loop), and goes deeper only as the person's wishes get specific.
- **A rail is a template, never the game.** A house title names a variant in level, action, character and world and the
  tools that build it; the person's game starts from it and is theirs.

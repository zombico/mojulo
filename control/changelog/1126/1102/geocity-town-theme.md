### The geocube's exaggerate: a town drawn by a hand that leans

- **`exaggerate` on the geocube** (`era/exaggerate.js`), a style word beside `register`: how the art director bends the
  shape of what the cube's builders build, as the register says how it is inked. A name (`storybook`, `crooked`,
  `street`), `{ from, …changes }`, or the terms: `lean` (degrees off plumb at a foot), `sway` (metres between the turns
  of the lean), `sag` (eaves and ridges out of level), `reach` (the height past which the lean stops growing: a cottage
  slumps, a tower sways), `elements` (the language caricatured), `kit` (`chunk`, `lean`: the street's furniture) and
  `seed`. Absent, every byte as it was.
- **The plan is law, the shape is free.** The bend is one seeded field over the world, zero at each building's foot and
  growing with height, so streets, lots, pads, colliders, anchors, doors and routes are untouched by construction, and a
  wall two buildings share moves once (a terrace never cracks). Laid after the tiles, so the courses lean with their
  wall; the faces re-lit on their bent normals. Its laws advise: `foot` (what a door's head moves), `fold` (under 1 the
  bend can open no crack and push no two buildings together: a gap g stays at least (1 − fold)·g) and `plumb`.
- **The geocity bent**: its masses, night panes and an evocation's facades on their feet (a construction frame stays
  plumb under its scaffold). **The street's furniture exaggerated** hardest (small things exaggerate most): the geocity
  kit's lamps, signals, poles, bins and hydrants chunked, its lamps and signals leant over their reach; a geotown's
  lamps, signals, signs and hydrants chunked and leant. A geotown's houses stay as they were.
- **A loud shopfront** (`exaggerate.front`, `geotown/front-loud.js`; the `street` hand carries one): a shop's front as a
  toy street draws it, the same shop pushed. Fat frames and mullions proud of glass set deep, a kick plate in its own
  ink, the door in fat jambs; the sign too big and a little askew in a dark rim, its letters blocks that read against
  it; the awning striped with a scalloped valance; posters slapped on the glass; ink splashed across the front; every
  colour pushed toward saturation. Each shop is its OWN: its walls leant to a hue of their own and a false front of its
  own form (stepped, an arch, a gable, battlements), neither its row neighbour's. Two principles hold on it: **no
  window square** (`skew`: every light's corners drawn inward, at least three off 90°, its frame, bars, sill and lintel
  warped with it, the shop's glazing and its storeys' lights alike; `speak`'s `skew`) and **no two connected buildings
  one height** (`step`: the false front raised until it stands that far from its neighbour; the `rooflines` law). The
  door and the plan stay where they were.
- **A loud street** (`exaggerate.streets`, `geotown/street-loud.js`; the `street` hand carries one), by the shops'
  principles over a plan that never moves: the walks laid in big pastel slabs over their own ground, cut on skewed lines
  (their ends too: no slab square), each a tint other than its neighbour's, set in a grout of the walk; the kerbs in
  candy stripes a metre long; the roads splashed with ink; the lines fat (`paint`) and the yellow pushed; a crossing's
  bars parallelograms; each hydrant, bin, post box, shelter and seat its own ink, never the last of its kind's; and
  no two neighbouring streetlights one height (`vary`, their wires strung to each pole's own top).
- **The street's doodads round** (`geotown/doodads.js`), a cube's exaggerate drawing them: a hydrant on its flange with
  its barrel, domed bonnet and nozzles; a bin a drum under its lid; a post box a pillar under its dome and slot band; a
  streetlight a tapered standard on its collar, its arm and its bell; a sign's and a signal's posts round. In the cube's
  TOON register (a cel register, or bands and an outline) each piece is EXAGGERATED and INCONGRUENT: every part sized by
  its own dice and knocked off its body's line (a mushroom bonnet on a squat barrel, a bottle of a bin under a cone, a
  standard bent in an S under an oversized bell), no two alike. Each stands on its foot where it stood (an S-bend's top
  back on its axis, so wires still meet their pole). So are its bike rings (a round post, a ring round it), its
  shelters (round posts under a vaulted roof, its glass, a round bench) and its cars at the kerb and on the drives (toy
  cars: a shaped body, a domed cabin, round wheels, headlights), each inside its plain one's footprint; a toon register's
  ring too big and tipped, its roof tipped and bulging, its car stubby, its wheels too big or too small, its cabin a
  bubble. A plain cube's furniture is as it was.
- **The street's signs and signals** (`geotown/signs.js`), a cube's exaggerate drawing them: a signal a housing on its
  backplate in a bright border, its three lenses each under its visor, on a round mast arm; a stop sign an octagon in its
  white rim, its letters, its back, on a round post; a street-name sign two lettered blades in their rims under a cap.
  And the signs an exaggerated street adds: a speed limit at the start of each walk along a street, a crossing's diamond
  ahead of each signal, a parking sign at the head of each parking lane. In the toon register a signal's lenses each its
  own size and its visors long and drooping, its head tipped and its arm bowed; a sign too big, squashed, turned in its
  plane on a bent post.
- **A study of receptacles** (`geotown/receptacles.js`, a shelf by group: litter bins, dumpsters, donation bins; the
  spike `scripts/spikes/geotown/receptacles.mjs`): each kind true to its size in the realistic register (a wire-mesh
  basket, a domed drum, a liner bin on its post, a wheelie bin, a recycling station, a heritage bin, a solar compactor,
  an ashtray bin, a concrete surround; front- and rear-load dumpsters, a roll-off, a skip, a four-wheeled container, a bag
  skip; a clothing drop, a book drop, a bottle igloo, a textile bank, a drop shed), then the same kinds in the askew
  register. Not yet placed in a town. (The World's hull ink smears thin and open parts: the askew study draws them
  under banded light alone.)
- **The language caricatured** (`elements`, 0 to 1; `exaggerateAnchor`, not yet worn by a town): every building
  element's number pushed away from the middle of its rail, inside the rail; doors and a household's rhythm kept.
- **The style guide** draws the cube's exaggerate as a card.

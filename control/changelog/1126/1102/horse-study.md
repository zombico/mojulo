### A horse study: a hero-grade horse with true gaits (spike)

A realistic horse for mounts in games, scenes and films, beside the fauna horse rather than over it (the fauna horse's
rows stay byte-identical). Its own skeleton with the passive mechanics as laws (the reciprocal apparatus, the fetlock's
sink, the scapular sling), gaits held to Muybridge and the published kinematics (walk, trot, canter on both leads,
gallop, rein back), the transitions, the halt, the jump and the idle, then a body in the realistic hero's manner over
it. Solo; the saddle and the rider follow, their sockets carried from the start. Nothing built yet.

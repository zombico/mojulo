### Destructible physics in the geocube: a cube says what its world is made of

A physical world asked of a geocube now breaks by what the cube's style says things are made of, so a thing's matter
agrees with how it looks.

- **The cube's `matter`**, by role (wall, floor, accent, trim, roof, ground, wood, metal, glass): each a matter or
  `{ matter, fill?, porosity? }`. Where the cube names none, it is read off the cube's own look: its surfaces' tiles (a
  stone-brick wall is stone, a flagstone floor stone, rock stone, wood timber), its interior kit (a research lab is
  concrete and steel), and the material words its styles use (ashlar and rubble stone, hand-brick and wire-brick brick,
  adobe, boards and thatch pine, slate stone, precast concrete). Each role says why it is what it is.
- **A physical level in a cube** breaks its walls, pilasters and set pieces by the cube's matter; its props keep their
  own classes'. A level not physical, or in no cube, changes no byte.
- The geocube manual names the facet.

### Destructible physics: the stage's water floods, pools behind levees and fills with rain

- **A physical stage's lake and sea** now lie on its ground as water: laid level, and the physics' pools, so what falls in
  floats or sinks and a fire is doused in it.
- **It moves when something moves it**: a crater dug at the shore floods from the lake, a broken levee lets it through,
  and it runs until it is still. No water is made or lost.
- **Levees**: what stands on the land holds the water back and is overtopped when it rises past; the water's push breaks a
  board, a concrete wall holds.
- **Rain** falls at the weather's rate (drizzle to thunderstorm), real time by default; the level's `matter.rainTime`
  speeds it.
- The page draws the water the physics moved. A level not physical changes no byte.

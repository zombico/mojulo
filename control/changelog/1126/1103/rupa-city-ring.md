### Destructible physics: a geocity's near buildings break, the rest stays the skyline

- **A geocube's geocity takes play** (`entities`, `camera`, `pilot`, `play`, `destruction`, `matter`, `scars`, `wind`).
- **Under `destruction: 'physical'`** its buildings within a city block of where the walk starts, the nearest two dozen,
  break by blocks: walls and a roof, each as its finish is drawn (brick, stone, precast concrete, a tower's concrete
  frame), a flat roof a slab, coarse panels so a tower stays a few hundred blocks. They stand at minute zero.
- The rest of the city stays massing. A city not physical is drawn byte for byte as before.

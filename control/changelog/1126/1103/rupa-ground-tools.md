### Destructible physics: craters throw their dirt, the shovel digs, rivers run

- **A crater on the land throws its dirt**: clumps from loam and clay, none from dry sand, spray and a hanging dust
  cloud, as much as it dug, landing on the dug ground.
- **The shovel**: a cue's `dig` takes a scoop (a hand shovel's, a bucket's, an excavator's) out of the ground in front of
  a character or at a place and piles the soil beside them at its angle of repose. Bound to a key, a player digs; dig
  after dig the hole deepens, to bedrock and no further, and water finds it.
- **Rivers run**: a physical stage's river is moving water, held at its levels at both ends; a dam ponds it, a crater
  fills from it.
- A level not physical changes no byte.

### Destructible physics: trees that break, burn and fall in a storm

- **A physical stage's trees** built near the trail are now blocks: a trunk of green wood in courses and a crown of
  leaves and air resting on it, standing on their roots on raised land. The stamped and far trees stay scenery.
- **A blast breaks a tree, fire burns it, an axe fells it**, and **a storm throws it**: when the gust at its crown passes
  its break speed it turns downwind about its foot and lies along the wind, its crown beyond its trunk.
- **The level's `wind`** is the storm. A tree is never toppled as a loose pile: the storm is its only wind law.
- **Rain and snow** lean with the wind in a physical level, or under a geocube's wind. Every other world's weather keeps
  the heading it had.
- `breached` and `fell` speak for a building or a tree (a group of blocks); a block restored at the start says nothing.
- A level not physical changes no byte.

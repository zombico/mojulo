### Multiplayer fixes: what the couch left over

- The gaps the couch work left: a pickup walked back over counts once, solo too; players 2 to 4 take supplies, hold a
  point and score their own; the gunner aims by a reticle; and on a split screen each player's view pulls in against a
  wall, carries its own HUD, and draws the page's finish.
- A coin or an item walked back over counts nothing more, in a one-player level too. Every level with coins or items to
  collect now records them this way, so its recipe changes.
- Players 2 to 4 take supplies (health into their own health, ammo, a power-up, a gun), hold a point for their side, and
  score their own coins in their corner beside the team's count.
- A driver-and-gunner level's one screen is the driver's chase view, no longer the gun's scope, and the gun's aim shows
  as a ring in the gunner's colour.
- On a split screen every player's camera pulls in against a wall as player 1's does, and the views follow the players'
  corners (player 1 bottom left, 2 bottom right, 3 top right, 4 top left), so each player's HUD sits in their own view.
  The camera's own effect passes (the creases, the occlusion, the fog) still show in player 1's view only.
- The hill-climb registry dates the Hijack City movers' `movers` mechanic (11-27), which came in without a date.

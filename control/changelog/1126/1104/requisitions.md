### Requisitions: the director raises production work, the departments fill it

The design for running a game's production across subagents. The director (the person's agent) turns the approved
bible into **requisitions**, one for each piece of the vision (a level, the foe cast, the hero). Each goes to the
department that owns its parts and stays there. The department fills it with **orders**: the exact tool calls, each at
the tier of judgment it needs and with only the context it needs, reported back through one typed submit. A requisition
is **received** when what came back matches its contract (the machine gate) and **accepted** when the person approves it
(the eyes gate). When production's requisitions are accepted, the director composites the game. A quick game is one
requisition to a **soloist**, a department of one that owns the whole loop, so a small game and a large one run on the
same machinery. The ledger is kept apart from the recipe and adds nothing to any render or export. A host without the
capability sees the surface unchanged. Design stage: nothing here is in the code yet.

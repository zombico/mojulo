### What to build next now counts cutscenes, game flow and acting

- **The planning map knows about the new story features.** The internal map the studio uses to decide what to build
  next now includes a game's whole order of play (its title, cutscenes, levels and ending), cutscenes shot by a
  director's rules, slow motion and freeze-frames inside scenes, and characters who act their lines. Games that need
  these now count as makeable, and the one missing piece next to them, holding the game world still while a cutscene
  plays, is priced and ranked so it can be weighed against other work.
- **The practice line and the brawler registered.** Power gains `moveset`: `strike` (one swing a press) and `strings`
  (the combo manifest, the world's `arsenal`, the templates' kits, measured on the practice room). Its `combat` gains
  `brawl` and Space's `levelForm` gains `belt`, both `local` in one package, `brawler`: the brawler's sim, crowd and belt
  are built as their own, reached from a greybox, not yet from a gameworld. Soul gains `mount` (`none`, `horse`: the
  world's `mounts`, the mount lane). Open's note now says what the world map's zones do (a page swapped at a gate) and
  what they do not (stream a region).
- **The probes.** Two new ones stand for the action game's templates: stylish action (makeable) and the open-world
  action-adventure on horseback (a gap: `space.topology=open`). The co-op street brawler now asks for the belt and the
  brawl, and reads a gap where it read makeable on a trail.

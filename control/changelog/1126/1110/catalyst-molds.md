### Catalyst molds: a mold joins a chapter, and an ask says which kind of gap it is

The design for bringing the catalyst shelf into the studio. A making catalyst is a mold: it teaches an agent how to
press a want into a mojulo-shaped thing. The studio works a game one unit at a time, and a unit's author is handed its
chapter's cards; the molds were not among them. Designed, not built: nothing below is in the code yet.

- **A mold names its chapters** (`chapters` in a catalyst's frontmatter, checked against the chapter registry; `[]`
  for one that molds for no game chapter). Every curated catalyst states it.
- **A unit's author is handed its molds.** A unit's view lists the molds of its chapter beside its cards, the
  operator's own among them, and a job reads one from its seat through `design_game`.
- **A job's ask says which gap it is:** a mold makes it, mojulo cannot yet (named in the cosmos registry where a value
  names it), or the remit is off. The agent sorts; the server only looks up what the ask names.
- **The readouts sort them,** and the director's next move follows the kind. A build ask moves nothing: the unit is
  received over it, as before.

### A drawn plate: solid forms through a two-point station, toned by superposed curves (spike)

- **A spike, not yet a kind or a door: `control/scripts/spikes/plate/`.** It asks whether the old sketch expander's
  aim (chiaroscuro from shapes laid over shapes, two-point perspective) can be said as a few principles an agent
  already holds, and drawn as curves rather than angular polygons. The agent stands forms on the ground in metres
  (the studio's own field terms, what `mint_solid` reads), names where it stands and where the light is; the eye, the
  horizon and the two vanishing points are one camera, each tone step is the closed outline of "darker than this"
  laid translucent over the lighter one, and a hatch is a slice of the form by a plane square to the light. Three
  finishes (wash, engraving, woodcut) come out as one SVG of cubic paths, the same bytes on every run, and sit in a
  book through the press's existing `plate` block (`grimoire.mjs`). Findings and what it would take to make it a
  kind are in the folder's README. Nothing on the tool surface, in the recipes or in the existing expander changes.

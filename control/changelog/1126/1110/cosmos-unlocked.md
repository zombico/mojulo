### What to build next now counts November's work, and says what that work made

- **The planning map caught up with what November built.** The internal map the studio uses to decide what to build
  next had fallen behind the work merged since the start of the month. It now counts as built: blows that knock
  things back, worlds that break (from a few crates up to matter that breaks by what it is made of, with fire, craters
  and floods), breaks the game or the player sets off, the special moves that carry a hero through the air, guarding
  and parrying, a hero's motion tuned from a card, levels written as maps, a platformer's course sized by its hero's
  jump, a world map crossed zone by zone, worlds joined in rings, a level's scenario and the things level design
  places on it, the screen a game is made for, a mascot as the hero, the kinds of body a game puts in it, a game's
  title screens and menus in its own look, the colour grade over a game, and a big moment taking the camera as well as
  the clock.
- **Things that were wrongly listed as missing are corrected**, and notes that had gone stale now say what is true:
  the fighter is fought on any level that names a lane, an RPG's party has its menus and shops, a vehicle comes from
  the registry by its word, and a flight's aircraft is still a placeholder shape although the real airframes exist.
- **Four gaps are now priced so they can be ranked.** Seventh-generation looks (the world, and the cast, which needs
  the world first) had no price and so could not be compared with other work; they have one now. Sound for a designed
  game is listed as a gap: a quick game can say its music and effects, a designed one cannot, and no level uses the new
  sound-effects library yet. A level's own HUD, talk menus and pause do not yet run on the shared game UI. Playing in
  a headset works for stage levels and is listed as part done.
- **Four more kinds of game to check against:** a 3D platformer, an action RPG on a world map and a destruction
  sandbox all read as makeable today; a headset adventure shows the gap it has.
- **The page now says what recent work made.** Each capability on the map can say the day it was built. The page
  counts, over the same rolled games it always uses, how many are makeable only because of what the last fortnight
  built, and for the games that use a new capability and still cannot be made, which missing piece they wait on most:
  the other half of a combination, and so a good next build. It also lists pairs of new capabilities that now work
  together in one game.
- The comic hero pack's game types now ask for knockback and breakable streets directly, since both are built.

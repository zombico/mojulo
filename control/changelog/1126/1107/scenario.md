### A level's scenario, beat by beat, and how much happens in it

- **One list, each beat whole.** A sketched map takes `scenario: { pitch?, beats }` in place of a concept: each beat its
  segment and its tile there, its kind (arrival, vista, meet, fight, traverse, find, rest, secret, climb, climax), its one
  line, its foes (`{ <bestiary id>: how many }`) or its find (`item`), what it teaches, how it feels (`pace`: calm,
  tension, peak, rest) and whether winning it opens its page's ways out (`opens`). It is read into the concept and a
  mark a beat on its tile (the mark names its beat), so the guide, the fights, the first look and the laws read it as
  they read a concept, and nothing pairs a beat with a mark by its kind or its order. The segments' sketches keep their
  land and scenery. A beat that is not whole is refused with words (a fight with no foes, a find with no tile, `opens`
  with nothing to win).
- **Every beat does something in play.** A fight stands its foes on its tile; a find or a secret that names its item is
  picked up there into the bag (a `collect`; the store carries the bag); a meet's or a rest's line is said on reaching it,
  and a find says what was found; a beat that opens holds its page's ways out, and a page holding the walk's goal holds
  that too, so the climax is won by its fight, never walked past.
- **The pace.** `level/pace.js` walks each beat from the last along the map's own paths at a hero's run (7 m/s): each
  beat's metres and seconds, each segment's beats a minute of walking, the longest stretch with nothing in it. Three
  laws join a scenario's: `dead-air` (no stretch over 30 s), `pace-shape` (tension before the first peak, a breath after
  each peak, the walk ending on its peak) and `segment-interest` (each segment on the walk 1.5 beats a minute at least).
  The Old Keep, rewritten as one scenario of nine beats, holds them all, and the measure says what the miniature hid:
  the town and the fields are crowded (a beat every 3 or 4 seconds at the run, 24 m tiles), the woods and the keep a long
  run between beats.

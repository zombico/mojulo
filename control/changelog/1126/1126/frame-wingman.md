### A frame's wingman

- An operation can field allies (`goal.operation.allies: [{ id, name, build?, palette?, ap?, lead? }]`, up to three):
  frames on the pilot's side, stood beside the drop. A wingman's mind (the frame rule's `ally`) fights the nearest foe
  awake within its reach; with none, it leads the pilot through the operation's places in order (its reaches' and its
  scans'), waiting at each until the pilot has reached it, and past the last keeps by the pilot. It is hard to lose (its
  armour three times its build's unless it says otherwise), and its frame takes the field's gravity.

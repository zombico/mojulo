### A scan objective, and a frame level's way to its places

- The objectives have a `scan`: stay inside a ring while a readout fills (its clock runs only inside the ring and keeps
  what it had when the pilot steps out; `total: false` starts it again), then what it found is said as a banner and as
  an event (`objective:found { task, text }`) a script can answer. An operation names it as a task
  (`{ scan: [x, y], radius?, seconds?, found?, text? }`), its ring a beacon on the holo table as a reach's is.
- A frame level shows the way to each place its tasks name (a reach's, a scan's) on the guide's line, an arrow, the
  metres and a pillar of light where it stands, held back until its task begins and passed as it is met; its line sits
  under the objectives' own (the guide's `top`). The guide reads a step's `when` as a task in hand reached and a
  `from` gate; a guide without them keeps its bytes.
- The operation's briefing lights a place's beacon by its task's number, as the table names it: a reach after a fight
  was never lit.

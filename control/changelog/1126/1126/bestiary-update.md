### The bestiary: foes as bodies with attack cards, lit by their level, a cast of its own for each rail

- Planned, not built. The walking action rails' foes are to be bodies (never a box or a mannequin), drawn under their
  level's light, with an idle, a run and a tell, strike and recover for each attack. An attack is to be a card of its
  own, so one body takes several, and each rail is to field a family of foes no other rail fields, starting from Fable
  of Artesia.

### Prospecting: a find revealed, the specimens a prospector finds

- The specimens (`prospect/specimens.js` `SPECIMENS`): what a prospector finds in space and on Mars (carbonaceous
  chondrite, nickel-iron, platinum-group metals, water ice, hematite, olivine, quartz, corundum), each with its name, its
  grade, what it is good for and its colour, and its wireframe drawn by mojulo's own depictors: a crystal's natural
  habit (`polygonizer/crystal-optics.js` crystalPolytope), a native metal's cubic form (a cube cut back by an
  octahedron, or the other way), a rock's creases (`form/land-stones.js` stoneForms). A new find is a row.
- A find revealed (`scene/channels/find-reveal.js` `FIND_REVEAL`): a frame operation's scan that names a `specimen` (and
  its `amount` and `unit`, the yield reckoned) stops the game as it is met (the world frozen, the keys held) and shows
  it: the specimen laid line by line until it stands whole, then turning and glowing in its colour; "Sample confirmed",
  its name, its grade, its yield counted up, what it is good for, logged to the survey; a chime rising as it is laid.
  It stands a few seconds, or until a key, then the play goes on. In the level's look; a page with no finds carries
  none of it.
- The Gnat drone larger still (some 12 m nose to tail as drawn) and tougher (twice its armour).

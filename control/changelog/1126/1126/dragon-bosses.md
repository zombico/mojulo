### The dragons: a family of bosses that share their idioms

- **A family of dragon bosses** (`boss/dragons.js`): five dragons the boss director fights (Ember, Rime, Storm, Venom and
  Gloom), each a human boss of the shelf whose laws hold. They share their IDIOMS: a tail swept at the legs, wings beaten
  down round it, a breath poured across the floor, a dive from the air, each told in its element's words; and each adds
  its SIGNATURE as it is hurt (fire rain, ice lances, a lightning lock, venom spat, a shadow swoop), quickening over three
  phases. `dragonBoss(id)` is its boss row, `dragonFigure(id)` its body (the draconic kin on two legs with wings, its
  colours its element's). A new dragon is a row.
- **A tell language** (`boss/bosses.js` ANSWERS, a move's `answer`): what the body does about a move, its hazard's tell
  drawn in that answer's colour, the same in every boss that says it: roll out of what cannot be blocked, jump what
  comes low, back off from what reaches round it. A move with none is told in its boss's own colour.
- **The dragons' snouts** (`creature-heads/derive.js` `drake-*`, from the dragon's skull): Ember's deep, square furnace of
  a snout with raised nostril bosses; Rime's short, high, pointed face under crests swept back off its brows; Storm's long,
  narrow blade finned down its bridge; Venom's broad, flat shovel with spikes off its wide cheeks; Gloom's long snout
  hooked down at its tip, tusks at its lip. Each drawn and checked all round.
- **The dragons' primary forms** (`figures/dragon-primary.js`, `gameworld/beast-boss.js` `dragonPrimaryFigure(id)`): each
  dragon on four legs, the beast its two-legged form rises from: a monitor lizard's body and walk, grown to a beast's size
  and bulk (its trunk barrelled, its neck and limbs thickened, its tail kept long), wearing what tells the dragon in either
  form: the same head (its snout, horns, crests and eyes, with its neck and jaw), the same wings (spread out off its
  shoulders), the same dorsal spines (in its horns' colour, down its neck, back and tail) and the same scales (the
  body's own light and dark kept, its value the dragon's).

### The dragons: a family of bosses that share their idioms

- **A family of dragon bosses that share one fight** (`boss/dragons.js`): five dragons the boss director fights (Ember,
  Rime, Storm, Venom and Gloom), each a human boss of the shelf whose laws hold. Every dragon fights in TWO FORMS: it opens
  on four legs (its tail at your legs, its charge, its breath), and hurt to 55% it RISES onto two (its `risen` look, the
  director swapping its risen figure onto its body), wings spread, and fights risen (its wings beaten down round it, its
  dive, its breath) with its SIGNATURE joined (fire rain, ice lances, a lightning lock, venom spat, a shadow swoop),
  relentless at the last. The IDIOMS and their answers are the family's; each dragon IMPLEMENTS them in its element's own
  pattern and words: Ember's breath a gout of flame swept across the arc, Rime's a cone of frost shards, Storm's walked
  down the line strike after strike, Venom's a checker of sick tiles, Gloom's black fire spat round where you stood;
  the tail a lash, a slam whose ice ridge runs out, a thunderclap rolling out. Its rise can bring an arena event (Ember
  sets the hall's rim burning). `dragonBoss(id)` is its boss row, `dragonLaws(id)` the family's laws over its own (one
  language: an implementation is a pattern its answer answers, `ANSWERED_BY`; two forms: each form fights only its own
  idioms, the risen phases wear the risen look). A new dragon is a row.
- **A dragon stands in a game** (`gameworld/foes.js`, a bestiary entry's `dragon`, or a routed family's `boss`): fought
  in its own lair as the hydra is (`gameworld/lair.js`), stood as a human boss of the director's with its two figures
  (`dragon: { id, form }` on the page, `gameworld/beast-boss.js` `dragonFormFigure`), closing to its idioms' reach between
  its moves, its class's weapon left off (its idioms are all it does).
- **A tell language** (`boss/bosses.js` ANSWERS, a move's `answer`): what the body does about a move, its hazard's tell
  drawn in that answer's colour, the same in every boss that says it: roll out of what cannot be blocked, jump what
  comes low, back off from what reaches round it. A move with none is told in its boss's own colour.
- **The dragons' snouts** (`creature-heads/derive.js` `drake-*`, from the dragon's skull): Ember's deep, square furnace of
  a snout with raised nostril bosses; Rime's short, high, pointed face under crests swept back off its brows; Storm's long,
  narrow blade finned down its bridge; Venom's broad, flat shovel with spikes off its wide cheeks; Gloom's long snout
  hooked down at its tip, tusks at its lip. Each drawn and checked all round.
- **The dragons' primary forms** (`figures/dragon-primary.js`, `gameworld/beast-boss.js` `dragonFormFigure(id, form)`):
  each dragon on four legs, the beast its risen form rises from: a monitor lizard's body and walk, grown to a beast's size
  and bulk (its trunk barrelled, its neck and limbs thickened, its tail kept long), wearing what tells the dragon in either
  form: the same head (its snout, horns, crests and eyes, with its neck and jaw), the same wings (spread out off its
  shoulders), the same dorsal spines (in its horns' colour, down its neck, back and tail) and the same scales (the
  body's own light and dark kept, its value the dragon's). Its risen form is grown to stand beside it (the same head and
  the same wings at the same size), towering over the beast it was.

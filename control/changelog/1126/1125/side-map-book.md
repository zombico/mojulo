### The side map in its game book

- A side map level has its page in the game book (`#sidemaps`, with its tab), read off the map as its level builds it,
  so the book and the game agree:
  - the side map's own elevation: its zones, its rulers, the bot's line through it, checkpoints, doors, ladders and
    pickups;
  - a row for each zone: its place and material, its run, its size in screens, what it teaches or which beat it is, its
    climb or drop, and its spawners, pickups and crates;
  - every law the map is held to, labelled as the machine gate.
- The level's own page shows a small plan of its zones in their places' colours, in place of a placeholder.

### The metroidvania box's map, revealed screen by screen

- A box's map is now revealed a screen at a time, as the hero enters each screen, the way a metroidvania's map is
  read. A room's screens join; the walls between rooms stand, with the doors through them. A gate is marked at its door
  until she holds what opens it, and a relic is marked where it lies until she takes it. Save rooms found and bosses
  still standing are marked, as is she.
- A minimap in the top right corner follows her through the box, the boss's bar under it in a boss's room. The pause
  shows the whole map walked so far, and counts the rooms seen.

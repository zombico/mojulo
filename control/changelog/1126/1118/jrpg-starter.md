### The JRPG starter: a party RPG from a town to its first boss

Planned. The aim is a first JRPG level-one made the way an RPG Maker game is made, from mojulo's own pieces.
- **The database:** a party, classes, skills, items, foes and troops.
- **The map:** a fortified town, a castle, a field and a dungeon, grown by the cubes.
- **The event:** townsfolk who talk in the talk box with their own faces, an inn, a shop, a fight and a boss.
- **Saving:** a save to come back to.

Planned in order:
1. The spine: a castle you walk, an inn in every town, and a world map that enters a town and leaves it.
2. Events with pages, in words.
3. The database's depth: states, traits, troops and foe patterns.
4. The menu and save slots.
5. The screen and the sound as words.
6. A staged battle screen.

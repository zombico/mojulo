### The airfield: a taxiway, its lights, the PAPI, and runways sited for their approach

- **A taxiway** beside every runway (`skyscape/ground.js`, `TAXIWAY`, `taxiwayOf`): parallel to it 85 m off its
  centreline, on the side toward the field's middle, joined by a connector at each end and one at its middle.
  - It lies on the runway's graded ground, with the strip between them painted the cover's pasture, the field it is cut from.
  - It is asphalt with a yellow centre line.
  - At night (`skyscape/night-lights.js`) its edges are lit blue every 30 m and the connectors' centre lines green.
- **The PAPI** (`papiOf`, the page's `night-lights.js`): at each runway end, four lamps beside the touchdown aim
  (`RUNWAY_AIM`, 150 m past the threshold) on the left as the runway is landed on.
  - Each is white seen from above its angle and red from under it (3.5°, 3.17°, 2.83°, 2.5°), so the 3° path shows two
    of each.
  - The page colours each by the eye's elevation from it, seen only from its approach.
  - They are lit by day as well as by night, so a map by day carries the lights channel with the PAPI alone.
- **Runways sited for their approach** (`APPROACH`): a runway's site now keeps the ground beyond the end it is landed
  over under a 3% (about 1.7°) surface, as it kept its departure's under 5%. Pilot School's island runway moved to such a
  site.
  - A clear departure still comes first: an approach is weighed only between sites that can be flown out of.
  - Only a powered craft's runway is held to it; a glider lands from whichever way its course brings it in.
  - Earthwork past 15 m now costs steeply, so a runway is not sunk into a cutting to find a clear approach: its walls
    would be obstacles of their own.
- **The lesson pilot** aims at the runway's aim point, the PAPI's. On its way in it comes down toward the slope's height,
  never under 90 m until lined up, and tracks the glide path more tightly. The night circuit is still flown to gold, and
  the flight proof checks that its final reads two red and two white.
- The night circuit's words name the PAPI and the taxiway lights.

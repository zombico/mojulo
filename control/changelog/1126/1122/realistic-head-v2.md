### The realistic head: a mainstream face

In progress. The realistic hero's head has a sound skull and a face that reads as a mannequin. This work reshapes the
face toward the proportions and the clean structure of a current game character, on the head's existing masses.

- The sex poles: a woman's jaw as wide as a woman's, her chin no longer a point; ears that lie flatter to the head;
  a man's lips and mouth less full.
- The mouth: the lips lie on the curve of the teeth instead of standing on a mound, their corners tuck into the cheek,
  and the profile's lips sit behind the line from the nose's tip to the chin.

Still to come: the planes between the features, the eyelids, the nose. Hair, brows and lashes are not part of it.

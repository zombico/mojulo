### Trees as flock

- **In progress.** Realistic trees' crowns drawn as a model maker's clump foliage is cut: a porous sponge the sky shows
  through and the sun lights from behind, its edges breaking into leaves, its shadow solid, dappled or off. A spike
  compares the crown as it is with the crown as sponge from the same cameras (`scripts/spikes/flock`).

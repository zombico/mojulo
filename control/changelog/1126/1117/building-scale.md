### Room to move: buildings laid larger for a third-person camera

A building's plan was sized for a walker (corridors three metres, five-and-a-half-metre rooms, three-and-a-half-metre
storeys, a two-metre stair with two of floor at each end), and under a third-person camera it read as cramped.

- **A core building's `scale`** (1 to 2, opt-in, `era/program-core.js`): its rooms, bays, corridors, core, lobby and
  annex laid that many times larger, each storey the root of it taller. Scale 1 is the plan as it was, byte for byte.
  A level's building, a geocube's or stage's building and a complex's building take it.
- **The `hall` stair** (`construction/circulation.js`): a building laid larger climbs flights three metres wide on a
  shallower going, with three metres of floor at the foot, the head and the landing, and a wider well.
- **Facilities are laid at 1.5**, and no longer ask for the fighting map's wide corridors on top of it. The Steel Serpents
  interiors (the holding block, level 1's barracks and hangar) are laid at 1.5, their laws still holding.
- **The room layouts' widths** are a body's with a camera behind it (aisles 2.2 m, lanes 2.2, pods 2, rows 1.6, a centre
  aisle 2.4), and a room is held to a hall's laws from 100 m², ten metres square.

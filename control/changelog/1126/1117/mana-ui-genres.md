### The beat 'em up, the run and gun and Fable of Artesia, drawn as games

The shooting rails' and the cinematic action game's way of drawing a game's screens now reaches three more kinds of
game. This work is UI only: no rule, number or system has changed.

- **The HUD stays inside the game.** On a page whose world is a fixed frame, the beat 'em up's and the action
  adventure's HUD used to land in the dark margin around it. Every game's HUD is now laid inside the frame.
- **The street: the beat 'em up and the run and gun.**
  - **The players:** each player across the top is a name and a health strip in cells in that player's own colour,
    on one soft shade instead of a box each.
  - **The run and gun's gun:** shown as a drawing of the gun, with its rounds as a large figure over a row of
    cartridges (∞ for the sidearm) and its grenades as dots. The machine gun, spread gun and flamethrower have new
    drawings, and the counter no longer flashes red with every shot.
  - **Danger:** alone, the screen's edges close in red as health runs low; in co-op, the low player's bar beats.
  - **The fight:** the foe you are hitting is named, with a short health bar, and a combo count grows at your side
    with a word at each step ("Nice", "Great", "Wild").
  - **The way on** is a pulsing "GO ➜" at the right edge instead of a word over the fight. The boss's name stands as a
    title over its bar, its phases as ticks inside it, and the stages and foes left are marks without labels.
  - **Scenes:** the HUD clears the screen while a stage's title card plays.
  - **The end of a street** no longer reports "health 0" on a loss, and the rank is stamped.
  - **Choosing a gun** before a street shows each gun's drawing beside its name.
- **Fable of Artesia (the action adventure).**
  - **Hearts** empty by quarters, so a hit that takes part of a heart shows it.
  - **The sword, the bow and the item** sit small at the top right. Guard, sprint and lock are no longer drawn as
    items; their keys are on the controls screen.
  - **Stamina** is a ring by the hero's shoulder, shown only while it is used and red when nearly gone.
  - **Lock-on** draws brackets over the locked foe's head, with its health under them. Before, the lock sat at the
    foe's feet, and a second bar was drawn above it.
  - **A round map** at the bottom right turns with the hero and shows the foes and the way on.
  - **Messages** like "No healing draught left" sit at the bottom left, off the middle of the screen.
- **The cinematic action game's work comes with it:** the bullet-time ring, the weapon wheel and the redesigned pause
  arrive in the same change.

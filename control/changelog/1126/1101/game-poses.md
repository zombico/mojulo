### The pose rails: how a game's people stand when they are shown

A character shown by a game no longer always takes the same tilted rest. A small table beside the body rails
(`control/lib/graph/gameworld/game-poses.js`) says which pose a context takes, by the game's rail and the role, from
the poses the door already has. The creator's own stand wins wherever it is said.

- **The cover**: the character bible's cover is a plate of the hero striking a showcase pose its rail picks (a comic hero
  `heroHips` or `fistToSky`, a fighter `fistCrush`, a person `pocketsCool`, a mascot `peaceWink`, a henchman crossing its
  arms), the world's seed choosing among the rail's own, photographed three-quarter.
- **The live stand and idle**: the bible's live page and its turnaround stand the hero in its rail's stand and play the
  rail's idle in place of the form's one breathing loop: a fighter's guard bouncing at the knees, a hero planted wide with
  the chest out, a person settling its weight side to side, a mascot bobbing with its head cocked. A world with no rail is
  drawn as before.
- **A showcase word as the stand**: the hero door's `gesture` takes a showcase pose (`heroHips`, `crossedArms`, …, each
  `…Left`) and holds its hit, solved on the rig with the hands on the body's own surfaces.
- **The cast lineup**: a cast's preview stands each member striking the showcase its role picks (the hero's side the
  rail's hero poses, a rival pointing the challenge, a mastermind turning its back or looking down its arms, the rank and
  file squaring up), no two neighbours alike, the right half mirrored so the line frames toward its middle. Stamped on a
  cast when it is first written (`lineup: 'showcase'`); a cast written before stands as it did.
- **The foes' portraits**: the cast sheet photographs each person foe striking the showcase its rank picks on the game's
  rail (a boss, an elite, a grunt), in memory: the foe as made never changes; a beast stands as made.

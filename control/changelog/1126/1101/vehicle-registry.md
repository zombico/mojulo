### Vehicle registry: one shelf of vehicles every game reaches, at the game's own fidelity

A game's vehicles come from several tables that share no names and read nothing of the game they are in: the city's
scenery nets, the physics classes, the highway fleet, the assembled rides, the race machines, the craft shelf and a
civilization's crafted technology. The registry names them once, the furnishings roster's way: one row a vehicle under
the words people say, claiming each table's key, the tables still measuring. A row says where it goes, what it is for,
the eras it belongs to and how a game meets it. A game's fidelity is read from what it already says (a realistic hero,
the juice's drawing, its setting), so a game with realistic characters drives a realistic vehicle and a kart race keeps
its karts. A civilization's technology names registry vehicles that can be driven, not only drawn on the bible's plates.

- **Stopping point:** planned, not built. Nothing a game mints changes yet.

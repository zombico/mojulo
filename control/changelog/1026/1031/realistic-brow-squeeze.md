### The realistic face squeezes its squint

- **The squint's squeeze on the realistic face** (realistic laws 15, `relational-face-rig.js` `SQUINT_SQUEEZE`, the
  head's `squeeze` relation): as the anime head's squint slides its brow's mass down onto the eye (`anime-sockets.js`
  `rise`), the realistic squint slides the mass over each eye, the brow and the forehead's foot, 3 mm down and a touch
  forward, so the skin between the brow and the lid's crease gathers while the lids, their corners and the lashes stay
  as the squint draws them; the brows' hairs ride it. The eyeSquint target and its sides carry it. A slide weighed about
  each globe rather than a drive through the head's field: the brow pad's dip moved the eye's inner corner, a valley
  where the pad meets the lid, four times as far as the brow, and the skin folded there. A row stamped before laws 15
  squints as it did.
- **hero-live's clay on the realistic hero**: the clay toggle swaps in a material of its own instead of stripping the
  painted one, whose parallax shader samples its colour map (stripped, the shader failed and the figure vanished).

### One body for every tiered hero

- **The form body** (`hero-form.js` `FORM_BODY`, `formBodied`, hero laws 21): one body for every tiered hero, whatever
  head it wears; only the head makes a difference, the body by its cast's sizes and proportions alone. It is the anime
  adult's body since laws 20 (rounded, the rib cage narrower under the shoulders, no male pectoral layer), named for
  what it is (`anime-looks.js` `ANIME_FORM_BODY` is it), and the landmark head's adult wears it now too: the same
  rounding, the same chest, and on the lit export the engine's smooth normals over the whole figure, the western face
  smooth with it. A row stamped before laws 21 keeps
  its body. Not yet on a hero robot's toy body, an undead's or a beast-kin's, nor on a child or a chibi (its build's).
- **The rounded female's hip read as the plateau it is** (`FORM_BODY.coreBands`): rounded, the female's hip side falls
  from its waist ring nearly straight to the trochanter, so its widest stretch is a plateau under 2 mm flat from the hip
  joint down; the core advice reads its peak anywhere on it instead of warning that it stands above the trochanter.
- **The stretch audit reads a rounded body** (`hero-stretch.js` `shellRings`): a rounded body's new points carry their
  edge's first corner's station and slot (`mix`, `body-smooth.js`), and the audit's ring map took them in the corners'
  places, so on a rounded body (the anime adult's since laws 20) its rings were midpoints and its seams read nothing.
  The new points are on no ring now.

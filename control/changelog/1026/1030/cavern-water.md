### Cavern water: lakes, rivers and falls underground

- **A river underground** (`trail.ground.cavern` with `water.river`): it runs between the cavern's end walls, rising
  out of the rock at one end and sinking into it at the other, a dark mouth in the wall at each; before, its water
  climbed the end walls to the cavern's rim.
- **No sea under the rock**: a coast on a cavern's trail (the cube's or the trail's own) is skipped and named in the
  water's forms (`skipped`), as a lake with no room is; before, the sea's level was read off the raised wall and its
  sheet hung inside the cave.
- **Underground water reads as underground**: in a cavern, the water (a lake, a river, a pool, a crossing's stream) is
  deepened toward the dark and shows less of its bed, so only the cavern's lamps, cracks and skylight light it; a
  fall's white water stays white. A trail without a cavern draws its water as before, to the byte.

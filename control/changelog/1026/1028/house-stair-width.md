### A house that names its width keeps its stair inside

A house whose recipe names its `width` and has stairs (every two-storey house the new-house draw stamps) took the
house's width for its stair's: `placeStairs` read the stair's width from the options the house rides in, so the flight
was laid as wide as the house and its well cut through the slab above, most of it outside the walls. The stair now
takes its own width (its spec's, else the stair default). Such a house renders with its stair inside, at its width; a
house that never named its width is unchanged.

### Animated face registers: the eye drawn, lit and moved as an animated character's

The mascot's face is made of lenses that hug its head: they read in a still, but a lid that drops in front of a flat eye
does not read as a round eye closing. This theme adds head registers, looks that standardized in 3D animation, routed to
by a facet or a brief word rather than a new argument on any tool, starting on the mascot's body and proportions:

- **globe**: the round eye with its lid on the face. The head is an ellipse and every feature is an egg on it. The eyes
  are lit eggs standing proud of the face, and a lid is a shell turning about its globe's centre.
- **browed**: the same globes, with the brow attached to the eye as the upper lid's rim, which bends to act.

The mouth is the same in every register. A mascot that asks for no register keeps today's face, byte for byte.

A worked example over the vehicle kind: the registered `sedan` type wearing
a `lowered` hull and `#1a1a2e` midnight paint — the silhouette sits closer
to the grid and the near-black body reads as a noir hero car. Orbitable at
the returned world URL; this is the eyeball-before-you-commit step of
populating a fleet.

This is a Door-1 recipe: pure params, nothing to load. Mint it as-is, or use
it as a starting point — `hull: 'chopped'` squeezes the glasshouse for a
meaner profile, `'wide'` plants it, a `taxi` type takes the same paint for
an off-duty cab, and the aircraft family swaps `decoration` for a livery
`scheme` instead.

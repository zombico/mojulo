# Make a 3D exhibit

Claude Code defers the mojulo tools' schemas: load the entry tool first with ToolSearch, `select:mcp__plugin_mojulo_mojulo__create_exhibit`,
and any other tool below the same way (or by a keyword search).

1. For their own people and time (a class, a gallery, an open day), call `create_exhibit({ start: {} })` first and produce it: one question a turn, their material read, never asked again.
2. Name the scale yourself, say back what their time holds, and make it only on their yes.
3. A plain "show me X" is made at once from a hall (card `exhibit`).
4. Never write an aim, a claim or a source for them: the knowledge is theirs. Show the person the contact sheet and let them judge it.

## Routes

From mojulo's routing cards: which call the ask goes to, and where it forks.

**An exhibit for someone's own people and time: produce it before it is made** (`create_exhibit`)

→ `create_exhibit({ start: {} })` FIRST when the person fits an exhibit to THEIR people and time: it builds nothing and answers the one question to ask (`producer.ask`). You are its producer; the knowledge is theirs. 1 ASK one question a turn, call again with what they said (`concept`, `people`, `time`, `brought`); what their own material answers is READ, never asked. 2 SCALE: name it yourself (`scales`, home first: subatomic, atomic, micro, human, macro, geo, planetary, space; history and idea beside the ladder); a concept across rungs is a room a rung. 3 FIT: say back what their time holds (`fit`, `map`). 4 MAKE on their yes, by `make.steps`: each room's world made NEW at its scale (the halls are samples, never the pick), its tour and curation, the rooms joined, the audit, its contact sheet (`contact`), their look. Never write an aim, a claim or a source for them. A plain "show me X" is made at once: card `exhibit`. Full family → `get_creative_toolset({ form: 'exhibit' })`.

**Make an exhibit: an animated science, space, earth, life or math study object** (`create_exhibit`)

→ `create_exhibit({ id: '<hall>/<id>', params })`, served live at `/world`. Halls: science, space, earth, life, math, history. Find one with `semantic_search({kinds:['view_vocab']})` or `get_exhibit({ hall })`; read its manual, `get_exhibit({ id })`, before `params`. Bio PROCESSES: `life/dna-process` (meiosis, conception, recombination, assortment), `life/energy-cycle`. A demo no exhibit covers is composed from laws: `science/classical`, `science/microscopic`, `science/quantum` (exact orbitals: not `science/atom`), `science/relativistic`. Its time series in real units → `get_exhibit({ ref })`. A historic city → `history/<culture>`. Stops a visitor steps through (a camera, a caption, the part lit) → `tour`; the expert's brief (aims, claims, sign-off) → `curation` (manual for both `get_view_vocab({ id: 'tour' })`). One exhibit at several settings, switched → `states`. Made exhibits as one gallery, a kiosk too → `create_exhibit({ rooms: [refs] })`; its check → `get_exhibit({ ref, audit: true })`. Keep a setup → `save_recipe`. Full family → `get_creative_toolset({ form: 'exhibit' })`.
